## Contributing workflow defects and correction

The prompt included complete historical Facebook posts and artwork briefs through `feedback_context`, along with recent-publication exclusions. All prior task outputs were passed to each downstream task. The observed candidate reused the excluded title, source story and prior promotional copy. Writer and Editor reported preserving supplied copy, despite their role instructions requiring writing/editing. The evidence demonstrates role/task noncompliance; it does not establish the model's internal reason for it.

The local correction:

- Keeps historical performance inputs to bounded outcome/metric fields; omits old post copy, asset briefs, and generated measurement instructions.
- Explicitly labels publication history as exclusions, never drafting instructions or current research. Source membership remains exact against the current snapshots.
- Restricts task dependencies. Writer receives research and creative context. Editor edits using research, Writer and Engineering findings. Tester then independently reviews Editor's final candidate. Delivery receives creative context and the final Editor/Tester outputs, not every earlier draft.
- Requires Editor to correct unsupported claims or replace an excluded topic, and Delivery to retain unresolved review findings. Approval gates remain authoritative even if Delivery omits blockers again.
- Returns redacted, bounded unmatched citation URLs and reviewer decisions in campaign diagnostics; existing generic error records are also enriched without modifying their artifacts.

## Controlled recovery

`POST /runs/{run_id}/revise-content` accepts `{"reviewer":"operator and reason"}` with the existing bearer authentication. It only queues a scheduled `awaiting_review` run before its original deadline, with fewer than three generation attempts and no publication attempt. It preserves the rejected artifact, tasks, sources and editorial context in `content_revision_history`; clears approvals and task results; and runs the full workflow again with fresh evidence. Concurrent requests can queue only one revision. Provider circuit pauses block the endpoint. Watchdog retries do not invoke content revision automatically.

Example after deploying the correction:

```sh
curl --fail-with-body --silent --show-error \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${MARKETING_API_KEY}" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data '{"reviewer":"Operator: historical-source context and final-review sequencing corrected"}' \
  https://crewai.bun2too.com/runs/76d7199a-8229-494d-be82-c81679ac0672/revise-content
```

The endpoint does not approve or publish content directly. October 21's window has expired and must not be reopened. October 22's window ends at 22:30 America/Chicago; if deployment misses that deadline, let the next scheduled campaign use the corrected workflow.
